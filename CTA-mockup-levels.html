Canned Tuna
LEVEL 1 — LABEL

The fastest dinners are the ones that never touch the stove. This Mediterranean tuna salad comes together in about ten minutes with pantry staples, and it eats like something you planned. Flake canned tuna into a bowl, fold in olive oil, lemon, capers, and red onion, then taste before you salt, because the capers usually get you most of the way there.

Serve it over greens if you want it light, or pile it onto toasted sourdough if you want it to count as a real meal. Either way, the whole thing lands on the table before a delivery app would have finished asking for a tip. (AD Canned Tuna)
