Coastal Style: John Moore, Creative Director of Outerknown

By Staff  ·  March 12, 2026

Hero Image

John Moore has spent more than a decade shaping the look of Outerknown, the sustainably minded label co-founded by Kelly Slater. His work sits at the intersection of surf heritage and contemporary menswear — relaxed enough for the beach, refined enough for everything else.

We caught up with Moore to talk inspiration, the evolution of coastal dressing, and what he reaches for when he's not in the water.

His go-to off-duty piece remains the button-down shirt — specifically, the Blanket Shirts — a style he's been wearing since the brand launched its first collection. (AdThis is a relevant ad for our readers from AdZen.buy a blanket shirt)

"It's the piece that travels best," he says. "You can roll it up, throw it in a bag, and it still looks intentional when you put it on."

Moore points to a broader shift in how surfers think about getting dressed — less performance-focused gear, more considered basics that work across contexts. Outerknown's upcoming spring line reflects that, leaning into natural fibers and earth tones drawn from the California coastline.
